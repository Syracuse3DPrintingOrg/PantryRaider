Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains what Forager, the optional hosted companion for Pantry Raider, knows about you, why, who else is involved, and what you can ask us to do about it. The Pantry Raider app you run at home is covered by the full privacy policy, which also lists the few things a self-hosted install sends out. For a self-hosted install without a Forager account, we hold no data about you at all.

Who we are

Forager is run by Dan Marafino, an individual doing business as Syracuse 3D Printing, in Syracuse, New York, USA. "We" and "us" on this page mean him. Write to support@pantryraider.app with any question or request about your data.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account: your email address, a password hash (never the password itself), and, if you turn them on, two-factor and passkey details (a passkey stores only its public half). Every sign-in, pairing, and kitchen token is stored only as a hash. We never ask for your name, address, or phone number.
  • Your kitchens: the name you give each linked kitchen, its app version and setup type, when it last checked in, and its remote-access address if you turned that on. No home network addresses are stored.
  • Your plan and usage: your plan, trial dates, and a monthly count of AI tokens used, so your allowance can be metered. For paid plans, Stripe's customer and subscription ids and the subscription status. Card numbers and billing details stay with Stripe and never reach Forager.
  • Free trial record: an anonymous id for the copy of the app that started a trial, so each install gets one trial. It holds no email address and stays after an account is deleted.
  • Cloud backups (Premium): the backup files your kitchen sends, newest three only. They are encrypted on the way to Forager but not on Forager's disk, so leave secrets out of a backup if that matters to you.
  • Recipes you share: what you chose to share (title, description, ingredients, steps, credit line, and photo), plus ratings and reports, which are tied to accounts so one person cannot vote or report many times.

Photos, AI, and recipe pages

Managed AI. Photos and text you send through Forager's AI pass through our server in memory to Google's Gemini API and are never written to disk, the database, or logs. Only the token count is kept.

Recipe photos. When you share a recipe that has a photo, your kitchen uploads the photo to Forager, which keeps its own copy so the recipe still shows its picture when your kitchen is offline. Before it is saved, the photo is re-encoded and stripped of camera details such as GPS position. Share pages and the community recipe pages on pantryraider.app load that photo from forager.pantryraider.app. If a recipe's photo is a link to another website instead, it loads from that website.

Recipe uploads. A recipe you upload on this website (typed, as a PDF, or as a photo of a recipe card) is read by the AI to make a draft you review. The uploaded file itself is not kept.

Share links and community recipes. A share link is a public page anyone with the link can open, showing only the recipe you shared. You can turn any link off from your account page. A community recipe is published for every Forager member to browse, with your credit line and never your email. When someone who is not signed in reports a share page, we keep a short one-way hash of their network address, never the address itself, and delete report records after 90 days.

Remote access. If you turn it on, your kitchen gets an address under forager.pantryraider.app. The secure connection from your browser ends at Forager's server, which passes the traffic to your kitchen over an encrypted WireGuard link. We do not log or inspect that traffic, but it does pass through our server, so it is not encrypted end to end. Use your own tunnel or VPN if that does not suit you.

Email. Forager only sends verification, password reset, and recipe share emails that you start. No newsletters, no marketing.

No tracking. No analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels, and Forager's server keeps no request logs of the pages you visit. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Where your data is kept

Forager runs on a virtual private server we rent in the United States. Its database, cloud backups, and recipe photos are stored there. If you use Forager from outside the United States, your data is transferred to and handled in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • AI photos and prompts: never stored.
  • Your account, kitchens, usage totals, and share links: while your account exists.
  • Cloud backups: the newest three; older ones are deleted when a new one arrives.
  • Recipe photos: while the recipe or share that uses them exists.
  • Expired sign-in links, codes, and sessions: deleted daily.
  • Recipe report records: 90 days.
  • Billing records: Stripe keeps payment and invoice records as long as the tax and accounting rules it follows require.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See the data we hold about you. Your account page shows most of it.
  • Correct it. To change your email address, write to support.
  • Delete it. Delete your account yourself from the bottom of your account page, or ask support. Deleting cancels any subscription right away and removes your sign-in details, kitchens, backups, usage history, and share links. Community recipes you published stay up, credited to "a former member".
  • Export it. Cloud backups download from the app, and on request we will send a copy of your account data in a common machine-readable format.
  • Object to or limit how we use it, or withdraw consent you gave.

Email support@pantryraider.app from the address on your account, so we know the request is yours. We answer within 30 days and do not charge for it, and using these rights never changes how we treat you. If you are in the EU, the UK, or a US state with its own privacy law, you can also complain to your data protection authority, though we would like the chance to fix things first.

Children

Forager is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold data about them. If you think a child has made an account, write to support and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we update the date at the top and email account holders before the change takes effect. Paid plans and the rules for shared recipes are in the Terms of Service.
